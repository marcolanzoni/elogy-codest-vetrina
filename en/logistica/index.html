The problem

Every new country adds another supplier.

Opening a market usually means rebuilding logistics from scratch. And cash on delivery has to be handled with each courier, country by country.

  • Another warehouseStock split up and tracked in more places.
  • Another courierWith its own rules, timings and portal.
  • Another contractTo negotiate, sign and manage, country by country.
  • Another COD setupEvery courier handles cash on delivery its own way, with its own rules and timings.

In Romania, Bulgaria and Hungary, at least six in ten online purchases are paid on delivery. If you don't offer it there, those orders go to someone who does.

Sources: Romania ARMO 2023 (orders) · Bulgaria Ecommerce Europe 2023 (transactions) · Hungary GKID-Mastercard/PwC 2024 (orders).

How cash on delivery works

Delivered, collected, reconciled. We handle it.

Your customer pays the courier. From there, we follow every payment, in every country.

  1. Ordered

    Your customer buys from your store and chooses to pay on delivery.

  2. Delivered

    The courier delivers and the customer pays at the door.

  3. Collected

    The payment moves from the couriers to us, from every country.

  4. Reconciled

    We match every delivered parcel to its payment, parcel by parcel.

What you see in the platform, for every shipment

  • Delivered
  • Collected
  • Reconciled

And if a customer refuses the parcel at the door, you see it coming back. It happens with cash on delivery: at least you know right away.

FAQ

What people ask us before they start.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No. Stock for the East sits in our warehouse in Hungary. Italy ships from our warehouse in Italy, Spain and Portugal from the one in Spain.

Can I start with a single country?

Yes. Start with one market, see how it goes, then add the others. Your store connection stays the same.

How do orders reach you?

Shopify and WooCommerce are connected directly, so orders come in on their own. For other platforms, there are our APIs.

What if a customer refuses the parcel?

It happens with cash on delivery. The parcel comes back and you see it in the platform. When we talk, we'll look together at how to handle returns in the countries you sell to.

How long does it take to start? And what if I want out?

Usually a few working days. There's no rigid minimum term: operational notice is all it takes to leave.
