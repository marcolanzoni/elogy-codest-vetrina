Cookie policy

Which cookies and similar tools these pages use, what they are for, how long they last and how to change your mind.

Last updated 2 October 2026

In short. We always use only the technical tools the page needs. The Meta pixel, which measures our campaigns, loads only if you accept it. You can change your mind at any time.

1. What they are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Other tools work in a similar way, such as the browser's local storage («localStorage» and «sessionStorage»). On this page we call them all «cookies».

This cookie policy covers the pages of the cash-on-delivery campaign run by eLogy S.r.l., Via delle Albicocche 25, 00071 Pomezia (RM), Italy. How we handle personal data is explained in the privacy notice.

2. Technical cookies: always on

They make the page and the form work and remember your cookie choice. If you do not accept measurement cookies, everything except your choice disappears when you close the tab. If you accept and later withdraw consent, we delete them from your browser. They are our own (first-party), are not shared with other websites and are not used for advertising. No consent is needed.

3. Measurement cookies: only with your consent

If you choose «Accept», or «Customise» with «Campaign measurement» ticked, the Meta pixel loads on the page. It tells us which ads on Facebook and Instagram bring visits and requests. Without your consent the pixel is not even downloaded.

With the same consent, when you send the form our server sends Meta the request event with your email, name and phone hashed (SHA-256). There is more on this in the privacy notice.

Meta also handles this data under its own privacy policy. The Meta group is also based in the United States: data may be transferred there, with the safeguards described in the privacy notice.

We use no other analytics or advertising tools on these pages, and the fonts are hosted on our own servers.

4. How you choose, and how you change your mind

  • On your first visit a banner asks what you want. You can accept, reject or customise. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
  • Until you choose, the Meta pixel stays off.
  • To change your mind, use the button below or the «Cookie preferences» link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads without the pixel.
  • You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. The banner will then come back on your next visit.

To learn more about interest-based advertising, see youronlinechoices.eu and Meta's ad preferences.
