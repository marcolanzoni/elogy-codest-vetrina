Questions we often get

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No, we have one. For the East, your stock sits in our own warehouse in Hungary, and ships from there to every Eastern market.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We do. We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

What if a customer refuses the parcel?

With cash on delivery it happens. The parcel comes back and you see it on the platform, alongside everything else.

Can I start with just one country?

Yes, and that's what we recommend. Open the first market, see how it performs, then add the next ones under the same contract and the same connection.

Does it work with my store?

Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly: orders reach us on their own, from every country. Other platforms connect through our API.

Am I locked in?

No rigid minimum term. If you decide to leave, the agreed operational notice period is all it takes.
