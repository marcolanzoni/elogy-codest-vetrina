Questions before you decide

Before opening a new country, it is normal to ask.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No, we have one. For cash on delivery in Eastern Europe your stock sits in our warehouse in Hungary, and reaches every Eastern country from there.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

What if the customer refuses the parcel?

It happens, especially with cash on delivery. The parcel comes back and you see it in the platform. On the call we look at how to handle returns in the countries where you sell.

Does it work with Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes. Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly: orders reach us on their own, from any country. For other platforms there is our API.

How long does it take to start?

Onboarding normally takes a few working days. For large volumes we plan the inbound stock together.

Am I locked in?

No fixed-term lock-in. If you decide to leave, we organise the exit with the agreed operational notice.
