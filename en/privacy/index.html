1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is eLogy S.r.l. (ELOGY S.R.L.), Via delle Albicocche 25, 00071 Pomezia (RM), Italy. VAT and tax code IT01739520623.

For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to sales@elogy.io.

This notice covers the pages of the «cash on delivery in Eastern Europe» campaign and the «Tell us where you want to sell» form. For the rest of elogy.io, eLogy's general privacy policy applies.

2. What data we collect

If you fill in the form

  • the countries you want to sell in;
  • how many orders you ship in a month, as a range;
  • how you sell abroad today;
  • the platform your store runs on;
  • your full name;
  • your work email;
  • your store website, if you give it;
  • your phone number, if you give it;
  • your choices on the two consents: the privacy notice and, if you give it, business updates by email.

While you visit the page

We collect some technical data, even if you never open the form:

  • where you came from: the parameters in the link you opened (UTM parameters, Meta campaign, ad set and ad IDs, the «fbclid» code Meta adds to links) and the page that sent you here;
  • a random ID for your browser, which does not contain your name. Without consent to measurement cookies it only lasts until you close the tab; with consent it is kept longer;
  • your browser and device type (the «user agent»);
  • a one-way hash of your IP address: we never keep the address itself, we only use it to stop repeated submissions and abuse;
  • what happens on the page: the visit, opening the form, the form steps, the submission, button clicks, and whether you scroll halfway down.

If you accept measurement cookies, the Meta pixel also collects data about your visit to this page. Details are in the cookie policy.

Where it comes from

You give us almost all of it. The data about where your visit came from is in the link of the ad you clicked: Meta adds it to the page address.

Campaign measurement, explained

If you accept measurement cookies, two things happen:

  • the Meta pixel loads in your browser and records the visit, the form opening and the submission;
  • when you send the form, our server sends Meta the same event with your email, name and phone hashed with a one-way algorithm (SHA-256). Meta uses them to tell whether the request came from one of its ads. It never receives them in plain text.

If you reject, or make no choice, nothing from these pages reaches Meta.

A note on how requests are prioritised

When your request arrives, it is automatically sorted by priority based on your form answers (for example, how many orders you ship). This only decides who we call first: no decision with legal effects is made about you, and the person who answers you is always a human.

4. What is required and what is optional

To answer you we need: your answers to the questions, your full name, your email, and confirmation that you have read this notice. Without them we cannot get back to you.

These are optional: your store website, your phone number, consent to business updates and consent to measurement cookies. If you leave them out, your request is handled in exactly the same way.

6. Data outside the European Union

Some providers are based in the United States (ActiveCampaign, DigitalOcean, Supabase and the group Meta belongs to). To be safe, we treat any data entrusted to them as transferred outside the European Union. When data leaves the European Union, the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for companies certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or, where that does not apply, on the Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards at sales@elogy.io.

7. How long we keep it

  • Contact request: up to 24 months from our last contact with you, if you do not become a customer. If you do, the data moves to the contract and follows the periods set by law (for example, tax rules).
  • Business updates: until you withdraw consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Technical visit data (events, source of the visit, hashed IP): 13 months.
  • Data stored in your browser: without consent to measurement cookies it disappears when you close the tab; with consent it stays until you delete it or withdraw consent. Meta cookie lifetimes are in the cookie policy.

8. Your rights

At any time you can ask us to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • erase it;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • receive it in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw a consent, without affecting what was done before. For emails, use the link at the bottom of each message; for cookies, the «Cookie preferences» link at the bottom of the page.

Write to sales@elogy.io. We reply within one month.

If you think we are handling your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or to the authority in the country where you live or work.

9. Other things to know

A form for businesses. The form is meant for online stores. We do not ask for sensitive data (health, opinions and so on): please do not include any.

Changes. If we change this notice, we update the date at the top of the page.
