The platform

Every shipment and its cash on delivery, under control.

Your customer pays the courier. From there, we follow every payment, in every country.

  1. DeliveredThe courier delivers, the customer pays at the door.

FAQ

What people ask us before they start.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No. Stock for the East sits in our warehouse in Hungary. Italy ships from our warehouse in Italy, Spain and Portugal from the one in Spain.

Can I start with a single country?

Yes. Start with one market, see how it goes, then add the others. Your store connection stays the same.

How do orders reach you?

Shopify and WooCommerce are connected directly, so orders come in on their own. For other platforms, there are our APIs.

What if a customer refuses the parcel?

It happens with cash on delivery. The parcel comes back and you see it in the platform. When we talk, we'll look together at how to handle returns in the countries you sell to.

How long does it take to start? And what if I want out?

Usually a few working days. There's no rigid minimum term: operational notice is all it takes to leave.
