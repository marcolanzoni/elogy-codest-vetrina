How we handle it

The shopper pays at the door. We handle the rest.

The shopper pays the courier. From there, we follow every cash-on-delivery payment, parcel by parcel.

  1. Ordered

    The shopper buys from your store and chooses to pay on delivery.

  2. Delivered

    The courier delivers. The shopper pays at the door.

  3. Collected

    The payment moves from the couriers to us, in every country.

  4. Reconciled

    We match every delivered parcel to its payment, courier by courier.

On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment. Parcel by parcel, in every country.

Questions

What brands ask us before they start.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No, we have one. Your stock for the East sits in our own warehouse in Hungary. From there it reaches the Eastern markets.

What if the shopper refuses the parcel at the door?

With cash on delivery, it happens. The parcel comes back and you see it on the platform. On our call we go through how returns work in the countries you sell in.

How long does it take to start?

Usually a few working days. For large volumes we plan the stock intake together.
