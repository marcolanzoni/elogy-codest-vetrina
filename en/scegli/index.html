What decision-makers ask

Before you open a door, it's fair to ask.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No, we have one. For the East your stock sits in our warehouse in Hungary, and reaches every Eastern market from there.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

What if a customer refuses the parcel at the door?

It happens, especially with cash on delivery. The parcel comes back and you see it on our platform. On the call we look at how to handle returns in the countries you sell to.

Does it work with my store?

Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly: orders reach us on their own, from any country. For other platforms there is our API.
