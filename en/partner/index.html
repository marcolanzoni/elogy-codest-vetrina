Why now

In Eastern Europe, people pay at the door.

In Romania, Bulgaria and Hungary, at least six in ten online purchases are paid on delivery. If your store isn't there, or doesn't offer cash on delivery, those shoppers buy from someone else.

Questions

What brands ask us before they start.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No, we have one. Your stock for the East sits in our own warehouse in Hungary. Italy ships from our warehouse in Italy, Spain and Portugal from the one in Spain.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

Does it work with my store?

Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly: orders reach us on their own, from any country. Other platforms connect through our API.
