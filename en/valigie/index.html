Before you decide

The questions brands ask before opening a new market.

Do I need a warehouse in Eastern Europe?

No, we have one. For the East, your stock sits in our warehouse in Hungary, and ships from there to every Eastern market.

Who collects the cash on delivery?

We collect it and reconcile it, parcel by parcel. On our platform you see the status of every shipment and its payment.

What if a customer refuses the parcel at the door?

With cash on delivery, it happens. The parcel comes back and you see it on the platform. On the call we'll go through how returns work in the markets you sell to.

Will it work with my store?

Shopify and WooCommerce connect directly: orders reach us on their own. For other platforms, there's our API.

Can I start with just one country?

Yes, and it's the smart way to do it. When you add the next one, you don't start over: same warehouse, same contract, same connection.

Am I locked in?

No rigid minimum term. If you decide to leave, operational notice is all it takes.
